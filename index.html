      Getting your App Password

      1. Open 2-Step Verification and turn it on, if it isn’t already.
      2. Open App passwords.
      3. Type a name like Mail Studio and click Create.
      4. Copy the 16-letter code Google shows, paste it on the left and click Save.
      5. Click Test connection. A green tick means you’re ready.

      On a company Google Workspace account and App passwords is missing? Ask your admin to allow it.
